03. Grid Independence Instant Solar

Integrated Current Transformer (CT) Sensor

Getting a government "green net meter" in Pakistan can take months of paperwork and high fees. NORA-3 integrates an ultra-fast CT sensor at your main breaker. It dynamically curtails solar output in real-time to match household demand, guaranteeing zero export to the grid without triggering reverse-feed penalties.

No Green Meter Delay · Microsecond Response · Zero Grid Back-Feed
04. Energy Storage Core 204V LiFePO4

High-Voltage Architecture & Unified Storage

Traditional 48V setups require thick, dangerous copper cables that overheat during heavy discharge. NORA-3 pairs directly with an internal 204V nominal Grade-A Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery pack in a fixed, non-expandable unified architecture. Higher voltage means drastically lower amperage, negligible resistive heat loss, and maximum round-trip efficiency exceeding 95%.
